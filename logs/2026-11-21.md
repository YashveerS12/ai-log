# AI Log — 2026-11-21

Did you know that early AI research in the 1950s used a toy called the “Logic Theorist” to prove 38 of the first 52 theorems in Principia Mathematica? The program, written by Allen Newell and Herbert A. Simon, was the first to use a form of automated theorem proving, showing that computers could handle abstract symbolic reasoning before even the internet existed.
