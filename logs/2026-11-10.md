# AI Log — 2026-11-10

Did you know that the first neural network, the Perceptron, was developed in 1958 by Frank Rosenblatt?  
It could only solve linearly separable problems, which led to the 1969 book “Perceptrons” that sparked a temporary AI winter.  
Modern deep nets can learn non-linear decision boundaries by stacking many layers, overcoming that early limitation.
