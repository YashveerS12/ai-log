# AI Log — 2026-11-10

In 2015, researchers trained a neural network on a dataset of 2 million images to generate new paintings, and the model’s output was so realistic that a museum curator mistook it for a human‑made artwork.  
The network, called a “GAN,” learned to mimic brush strokes and color palettes purely from statistical patterns, not from any artistic instruction.  
It sparked debate over whether AI can truly “create” or merely remix existing styles.
