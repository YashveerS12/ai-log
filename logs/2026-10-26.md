# AI Log — 2026-10-26

Neural networks can learn to play simple games by observing only a single opponent's moves, without any prior knowledge of the rules.  
This “imitation learning” was first demonstrated in 2018 with a robotic arm that mimicked a human hand using only video input.  
It shows that AI can reverse‑engineer complex tasks purely from visual data, a technique now used in autonomous driving and medical imaging.
