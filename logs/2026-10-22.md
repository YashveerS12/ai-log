# AI Log — 2026-10-22

Did you know that early neural networks used a learning rule called the Hebbian rule, inspired by the phrase “cells that fire together, wire together”? It’s a simple principle that underpins modern deep learning. In 1943, psychologist Donald Hebb formalized this idea, and it wasn’t until the 1980s that computational models began to implement it. This biological insight still influences how we design learning algorithms today.
