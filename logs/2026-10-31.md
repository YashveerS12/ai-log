# AI Log — 2026-10-31

Did you know that the first neural network, the Perceptron, was invented in 1957 by Frank Rosenblatt? It could learn to recognize simple shapes, but it was limited by a single layer, which led to the AI winter of the 1970s. In the 1980s, backpropagation was rediscovered, allowing multi‑layer networks to finally train and spark the modern deep learning boom.
