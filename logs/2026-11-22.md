# AI Log — 2026-11-22

Did you know that the first neural network was built in 1958 by Frank Rosenblatt and called the perceptron? It could learn to recognize simple patterns like letters, but it struggled with more complex tasks. That limitation sparked a 30‑year research slowdown known as the “AI winter.” In the 1980s, back‑propagation was rediscovered, sparking a resurgence that led to today’s deep learning boom.
