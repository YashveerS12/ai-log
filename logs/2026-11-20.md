# AI Log — 2026-11-20

Did you know that the first neural network, the perceptron, was built in 1957 by Frank Rosenblatt?  
It could learn to recognize simple patterns but was limited to linear separability.  
The breakthrough came in 1986 when backpropagation was popularized, allowing deep nets to learn non‑linear features.  
Today, AI models can even generate their own training data, a technique called self‑supervised learning.
